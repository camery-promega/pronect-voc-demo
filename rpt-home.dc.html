Timing conflict

At your current settings, MyGlo can't complete a read before the next one is scheduled to start.

To fix this, do one of the following:

  • Increase the read interval: adds time between reads without affecting your signal-to-noise ratio.
  • Decrease the integration time: shortens each read, but reduces your signal-to-noise ratio, so use this only if your signal is strong.

The Read Plate button stays disabled until this is resolved.
Timing risk

At your current settings, each read uses most of the time between reads. Your run will proceed, but there isn't much room if a read takes longer than expected.

To reduce this risk, do one of the following:

  • Increase read interval: recommended, since it doesn't affect your data.
  • Decrease integration time: only if your signal-to-noise ratio can tolerate a shorter read.

To continue as configured, select the Read Plate button.
